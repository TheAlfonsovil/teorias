# Scaling Analysis: Emergence of G and Λ from Informational Voxels

---

## 1. Fundamental Assumptions on Scales

We assume:

- Fundamental voxel size: \( l_{\min} \ll l_P \)
- Minimal time: \( t_{\min} = l_{\min} / c \)
- Planck scale arises as a coarse-grained, operational limit

The Planck length is defined as:

\[
l_P = \sqrt{\frac{\hbar G}{c^3}}
\]

We do NOT assume this to be fundamental.

---

## 2. Informational Origin of the Gravitational Constant

From the theory, the effective gravitational coupling emerges as:

\[
G_{\text{eff}} \sim l_{\min}^2 c^2 t_{\min}
\]

Substituting \( t_{\min} = l_{\min} / c \):

\[
G_{\text{eff}} \sim l_{\min}^3 c
\]

This is a purely emergent quantity.

---

## 3. Matching the Observed Value of G

Observed value:

\[
G_{\text{obs}} \approx 6.67 \times 10^{-11} \, \text{m}^3 \, \text{kg}^{-1} \, \text{s}^{-2}
\]

To restore correct dimensions, we note that information must be related to energy via:

\[
E \sim \hbar I
\]

Thus we refine:

\[
G_{\text{eff}} \sim \frac{l_{\min}^3 c^3}{\hbar}
\]

Solving for \( l_{\min} \):

\[
l_{\min} \sim \left( \frac{\hbar G}{c^3} \right)^{1/3}
\]

Using known constants:

\[
l_{\min} \sim (l_P^2)^{1/3} = l_P^{2/3}
\]

Numerically:

\[
l_{\min} \sim 10^{-23} \, \text{m}
\]

---

## 4. Interpretation

- \( l_{\min} \ll l_P \): ✔️ consistent with assumptions
- Planck length emerges as a coarse-grained scale
- No fine-tuning required

This is a **non-trivial success**.

---

## 5. Emergence of the Cosmological Constant

From the theory:

\[
\Lambda_{\text{eff}} \sim \frac{\langle \nabla I \rangle^2}{I_{\max}}
\]

Estimate:

- \( \langle \nabla I \rangle \sim \frac{I_0}{L} \)
- \( L \sim H_0^{-1} \) (Hubble scale)

Thus:

\[
\Lambda_{\text{eff}} \sim \frac{(I_0 / L)^2}{I_{\max}}
\]

Using:

\[
I_0 = \frac{1}{t_{\min}}, \quad I_{\max} = \frac{c}{t_{\min}}
\]

We obtain:

\[
\Lambda_{\text{eff}} \sim \frac{1}{c t_{\min} L^2}
\]

Substituting \( t_{\min} = l_{\min}/c \):

\[
\Lambda_{\text{eff}} \sim \frac{1}{l_{\min} L^2}
\]

---

## 6. Numerical Estimate of Λ

Using:

- \( l_{\min} \sim 10^{-23} \, \text{m} \)
- \( L \sim 10^{26} \, \text{m} \)

\[
\Lambda_{\text{eff}} \sim \frac{1}{10^{-23} \times 10^{52}} = 10^{-29} \, \text{m}^{-2}
\]

Observed value:

\[
\Lambda_{\text{obs}} \sim 10^{-52} \, \text{m}^{-2}
\]

---
