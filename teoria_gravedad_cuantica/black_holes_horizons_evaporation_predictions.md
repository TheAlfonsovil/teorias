## 15. Black Holes as Informationally Saturated Regions

### 15.1 Definition of an Informational Black Hole

A black hole is defined as a spacetime region in which the local information density reaches the maximal voxel capacity:

\[
I = I_{\max}
\]

In this regime, no additional information can be transmitted outward through local causal channels.

---

## 16. Emergence of the Horizon Area Law

### 16.1 Horizon as a Saturation Surface

Consider a compact region of radius \( R \) in which all voxels are saturated.

Information attempting to exit radially outward encounters saturated voxels and must be redirected tangentially.

The horizon is therefore a **two-dimensional causal bottleneck**.

---

### 16.2 Counting Horizon Degrees of Freedom

Each surface voxel contributes at most one independent information channel per minimal time step.

Number of surface voxels:

\[
N_{\text{surf}} \sim \frac{A}{l_{\min}^2}
\]

Total information capacity of the horizon:

\[
I_{\text{horizon}} \sim \frac{A}{l_{\min}^2} I_0
\]

---

### 16.3 Entropy–Area Relation

Using \( I_0 \sim 1/t_{\min} \) and coarse-graining to observable scales, entropy is proportional to information capacity:

\[
S_{\text{BH}} \propto \frac{A}{l_{\min}^2}
\]

Matching with the Bekenstein–Hawking entropy:

\[
S_{\text{BH}} = \frac{k_B A}{4 l_P^2}
\]

implies that the Planck length emerges as:

\[
l_P^2 \sim \langle l_{\min}^2 \rangle_{\text{coarse}}
\]

Thus, the area law is a **direct consequence of informational saturation**, not quantum gravity per se.

## 17. Informational Interpretation of Black Hole Evaporation

### 17.1 No Information Destruction

Since information is fundamental and conserved, evaporation cannot destroy information.

Instead, it corresponds to **causal redirection** of information flow.

---

### 17.2 Mechanism of Evaporation

Near the horizon:

- Radial channels are saturated
- Tangential channels dominate
- Small fluctuations create temporary unsaturated paths

These allow information to leak outward slowly.

This leakage manifests macroscopically as Hawking radiation.

---

### 17.3 Evaporation Rate

The evaporation rate is determined by:

- Horizon area \( A \)
- Maximal information flux \( I_{\max} \)

Leading to:

\[
\frac{dM}{dt} \sim - \frac{I_{\max}}{A}
\]

which reproduces the qualitative mass-loss behavior:

\[
\frac{dM}{dt} \propto - \frac{1}{M^2}
\]

---

### 17.4 Resolution of the Information Paradox

- Information is never lost
- It is redistributed along non-local causal paths
- Late-time radiation is correlated

The paradox is avoided without firewalls or non-unitarity.

## 18. Cosmological Implications (Brief)

The same saturation principle implies that early-universe high-density phases correspond to near-saturated informational states, while cosmic expansion corresponds to gradual desaturation and redistribution.

## 19. Predictions and Falsifiability

### 19.1 Fundamental Predictions

1. **No true spacetime singularities**  
   All singularities are replaced by saturated informational cores.

2. **Area law universality**  
   Entropy scales with area for any causal horizon, not only black holes.

3. **Modified near-horizon correlations**  
   Late-time Hawking radiation must show non-thermal correlations.

---

### 19.2 Observational Signatures

- Deviations from perfect thermality in black hole radiation
- Absence of trans-Planckian modes
- Universal entropy bounds independent of field content

---

### 19.3 Cosmological Tests

- Effective dark energy must evolve slowly with cosmic information gradients
- No large vacuum energy contributions
- Possible imprints in late-time acceleration data

---

### 19.4 Theoretical Falsifiability

The theory is falsified if:

- Information loss is experimentally observed
- Black hole entropy deviates from area scaling
- A fundamental minimal length equal to \( l_P \) is demonstrated

---

## 20. Final Remarks

Black holes, in this framework, are not exotic quantum objects but natural consequences of informational saturation under causal constraints.

Evaporation is a slow re-routing of information, and spacetime geometry itself is a macroscopic bookkeeping device for causal information flow.

This closes the conceptual loop between quantum mechanics, gravity, and cosmology.
